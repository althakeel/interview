Section A - Multiple choice (60 points)
Select one best answer for each question.
1. A premium skincare brand has strong sales in Europe but no UAE presence. What is the best first
step before approaching it?
A.
 Promise a nationwide launch
B.
 Assess UAE demand, competition, pricing and channel fit
C.
 Request an exclusivity contract immediately
D.
 Estimate revenue from European sales alone
2. You find a brand that says it is 'open to international expansion.' Who is the best initial contact?
A.
 Any retail store employee
B.
 Its social media followers
C.
 The founder or international partnerships decision-maker
D.
 A logistics provider that handles its shipments
3. Which combination makes an exclusivity clause commercially clear?
A.
 Territory, covered products, channels, duration and performance conditions
B.
 A verbal promise and a launch date
C.
 A brand logo and a marketing budget
D.
 A product catalogue and an email introduction
4. A brand asks for a large minimum annual purchase before sharing its UAE sales data. What is the
strongest response?
A.
 Accept to secure the opportunity
B.
 Refuse all minimum commitments
C.
 Ask for evidence and propose phased, measurable commitments
D.
 Replace the minimum with a higher royalty
5. A product sells to a retailer for AED 70 and its landed cost is AED 42. What is the distributor's gross
margin percentage on that sale?
A.
 28%
B.
 40%
C.
 60%
D.
 67%
6. Which evidence is most useful when deciding whether a new food brand can enter the UAE
successfully?
A.
 The founder's personal confidence
B.
 A competitor's social media follower count alone
C.
 A sales forecast with no channel assumptions
D.
 Category demand, target customers, price points, compliance and route to market
